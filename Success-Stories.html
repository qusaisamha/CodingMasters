Success Stories of Programmers

1. Cyril Samir: From a juice seller to a successful programmer

Cyril Samir: From a juice seller to a successful programmer Cyril began his journey as a juice seller, but his passion for technology led him to self-learn programming. He exploited the Internet as a platform for learning multiple programming languages, and built himself a wide client base by offering his services on freelancing platforms. Cyril succeeded in achieving a good financial income and establishing a name for himself in the field of programming.

2. Mark Zuckerberg: founder of Facebook

Mark Zuckerberg: founder of Facebook Although he studied at Harvard University, Mark Zuckerberg did not complete his university studies to devote himself to developing Facebook. He started programming Facebook in his room, using online resources. Today, Facebook is one of the largest social media networks in the world, and Mark Zuckerberg is one of the richest people in the world.

3. Linda Lico: CTO at Microsoft

Linda Lico: CTO at Microsoft Linda Lico did not have a degree in the field of computers, but she taught herself programming through books and resources available in the university library where she worked. Today, she is one of the most important figures in the technology field and holds the position of CTO at Microsoft.

4. Elon Musk: Entrepreneur and businessman

Elon Musk: Entrepreneur and businessman Elon Musk is another example of someone who self-taught programming and achieved great success. He started programming games at an early age, and used this skill to found successful companies like PayPal, Tesla, and SpaceX
